A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Every detail should feel straightforward, from the first message to the final touch.

How do I get an accurate quote? +

Share the type and size of your space, the service you need and any special priorities. The final quote should be confirmed by the cleaning business before a booking is accepted.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies? +

That depends on the provider's policy and the needs of your space. Mention any product preferences or sensitivities in your request so they can confirm the arrangements.

Can I request a recurring clean? +

Yes, recurring service can be discussed. Select your preferred frequency in the request form and the team can confirm availability and terms.

What if I have a specific priority? +

Let the team know in advance. A short priority list helps set expectations and makes it easier to focus on the areas that matter most to you.
